# Anti-AI-Slop

Remove:

- generic openings;
- broad claims without an object;
- forced lists of three;
- artificial paragraph symmetry;
- buzzwords;
- transitions that carry no meaning;
- empty calls to action and conclusions;
- unsupported certainty;
- repeated melodramatic metaphors.

Replace a problem only with a detail from the source or brief. If no detail exists, cut the sentence or use a placeholder. Never fill gaps with model knowledge.

For each paragraph ask:

1. What is this paragraph's job?
2. What detail makes it specific?
3. Does the reader gain understanding or an action?
4. Could this sentence fit almost any topic?

Do not force a neutral tone onto an argument or flatten a deliberate voice.
