# AI Tell Lexicon — Indonesian Output

This is a detection aid, not a ban list. A word is a finding only when it is decorative or vague and part of a pattern. Do not replace precise technical terms to reduce a score.

## High-signal phrases

### Cliché openings

Examples include:

- `Dalam era globalisasi yang serba cepat ini...`
- `Seiring berjalannya waktu...`
- `Di tengah pesatnya perkembangan teknologi...`
- `Tidak dapat dipungkiri bahwa...`
- `Perlu diketahui bahwa...`
- `Di era digital ini...`
- `Belakangan ini, semakin banyak...`

### Inflated phrases

Examples include:

- `memainkan peran penting/krusial/vital`;
- `memegang peranan penting`;
- `tidak dapat dilepaskan dari`;
- `sangat fundamental`;
- `berkontribusi secara signifikan`;
- `menjadi kunci keberhasilan`;
- `solusi yang tepat`;
- `tantangan dan peluang`;
- `potensi yang luar biasa`;
- `lanskap`, `revolusi`, `paradigma`, `sinergi`, `ekosistem`, and `inovasi` without concrete meaning.

### Weak or corporate verbs

Watch for:

- `melakukan` + noun where a direct verb is available;
- `memberikan dampak/kontribusi/penjelasan`;
- `memanfaatkan potensi`;
- `mendorong terciptanya`;
- `menjadi wadah`;
- `berperan aktif dalam`;
- `secara komprehensif/efektif/optimal` without evidence.

### Structural signs

- three consecutive paragraphs start with the same connector;
- forced triads;
- rhetorical questions followed by self-answers;
- every paragraph ends with a summary sentence;
- identical sentence and paragraph lengths;
- title repeated as the first sentence;
- decorative bold, emoji in formal writing, or excessive headings;
- chatbot residue such as `Tentu! Berikut adalah...` or `Semoga membantu!`.

## Density heuristic

Count hits per 1,000 words:

- 0-2 hits without a structural cluster: low risk;
- 3-6 hits or one structural cluster: medium risk;
- 7+ hits or multiple pattern families in a paragraph: high risk.

AI detection is heuristic. Optimize for human readers, not detector evasion.
